SigmaQ - install in 3 steps

1. Double-click SigmaQ-Setup-0.1.0.exe
2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
      click "More info"  ->  click "Run anyway"
   (This appears because the installer isn't paid-for/signed. It is safe.)
3. Click "Yes" on the admin prompt, then "Next" / "Finish".

Open Ableton Live or FL Studio and search for "Sigma Q".
- Ableton: if it's missing, Preferences > Plug-ins > enable "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" > Rescan.
- FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.

To uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > SigmaQ.
